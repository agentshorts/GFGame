Acceleration Hop tutorial: Accel Hop 101 (issue 348)

Where: the hub (L_Hub). A new pink gate, "Accel Hop 101", stands beside the Beat Hop portal, past the jukebox. Walk in.
It opens a new map, L_AccelHop_Tutorial_v1, a copy of the Beat Hop test map. L_Beat itself is unchanged and the Beat Hop portal still opens it.
Before the map script has run on the build machine the gate says "Under construction", like any missing map.
A panel at the top of the screen walks you through four lessons along one straight course, one song and one beat for the whole run:
1/4 Hop on the beat: land on the thump 4 times (misses do not count, they do not have to be in a row). No lit tile yet.
2/4 Steer in the air: tiles zigzag left and right. Mid-hop, look where you want to land. 3 switches from one side to the other.
3/4 Hit the lit tile: the magenta diamond shows up from here on. Land on it twice.
4/4 Put it all together: strip, zigzag, strip. A chain of 6 on-beat hops (a miss resets the chain), at least one zigzag and one lit landing.
Each finished lesson flashes "Nice!" and moves on. Done says "Hop certified".
Walk ahead onto a later lesson's tiles and it skips to that lesson. Walking back never rewinds.
Two HUB gates: one by the start (behind you at the spawn) and one just past the last tile. Both take you back to the hub.
Sprint and stamina work here like on the world road.
Co-op: every player has their own prompts and progress. The lit tile is shared, as on the world road.

Acceleration Hop v3: aim steering and the lit-tile diamond (issue 347)

Where: any Acceleration Hop road on the world map (L_World_Factions_A).
Jump on the road and turn the mouse mid-air: the hop curves toward where you look. Speed stays the same, only the direction turns, up to 90 degrees a second.
The landing ring moves with the turn while you are still in the air. Hold the aim and you come down inside it.
A hop that takes off on the road now steers all the way down, even when a hard turn carries you off the side of the road, and even over the top of a double or triple jump. Before this fix the steering switched off as soon as the pawn left the road's music box (its sides are under 6 m from the road's middle and its top 4 m up), and the ring froze where the jump had aimed it. Try a sharp mouse turn mid-hop and a triple jump: the ring should follow the camera the whole way.
Look back over your shoulder (more than 150 degrees off your flight) and nothing turns, so a glance behind never spins you round. Very slow hops (under 1.5 m/s) do not turn either.
Off the road, air control is the same as before. The stick still nudges as it did.
The lit tile now has a big magenta diamond lying flat on the tile itself, with a black outline and a white core, about the size of the tile. It pulses with the beat. A magenta arrow floats above it so you can find it from far off or from the top of a hop.
The landing ring is drawn on top of the diamond and stays its own colors (green, gold over the lit tile), so the two never read as the same thing.
Tuning (designers): GF|AccelHop|AirSteer in the movement tuning asset (Accel Hop), GF|AccelHop|LitMarker on the hop road actor (Lit Tuning).

Acceleration Hop: your own music (issue 343)

Where: any Acceleration Hop road, plus Esc, Settings, "Beat Hop playlist".
Drop your own .wav files into Content/LicensedMusic/AccelHop (an editor checkout) or GFGame/LicensedMusic/AccelHop next to a packaged game. Git ignores both folders: never commit music.
Each track needs a BPM: put it in the file name ("Night Drive 128bpm.wav") or in a sidecar Night Drive.json with {"bpm": 128, "offset_ms": 40}.
mp3, ogg and flac don't play in game. Convert them with: python3 Tools/hop_music_prep.py add song.mp3 --analyse (needs ffmpeg; --analyse finds the BPM).
Step onto the road: your first track plays and the tiles pulse on its BPM. Hops judge on that tempo too.
Open the playlist: tick tracks in or out, move them Up or Down, pick In Order, Shuffle or Repeat One.
Resume on (default): leave the road and come back, the same track carries on where it stopped. Resume off: the next track starts.
Empty the folder, or untick everything: the built-in song plays as before.
Co-op: each player hears their own playlist. Nothing is shared.
The lit tile (issue 342) is the same for everyone and moves on at the road's own steady pace, not your track's tempo. Your music sets only your beat and your pulses. Land on the lit tile on your own beat and you get its boost.

Acceleration Hop v2: the lit tile (issue 342)

Where: the same hop road on the world map (L_World_Factions_A).
While you run the road, one tile a few hops ahead glows at full strength with a bright rim and a sharp pulse on the beat. A gold diamond floats over it so it reads from far off.
That is where to land. It is usually a Boost tile, sometimes a Downbeat, now and then a plain one. Never a green Landing tile.
Land on it on the beat and the boost is 1.5x what that tile would normally give. It then goes dark and the next beat lights a new one.
Miss it and it stays lit until you run past it.
Everyone on the road sees the same lit tile. The first player on the road (join order) is the one it is picked ahead of.
The landing ring now follows you all the way down: steer in the air or dash and it moves. It turns gold with a second ring when you are coming down on the lit tile.

Rest area start (issue 335)

Where: the world map (L_World_Factions_A). A new player now starts at the rest area halfway between Verdant and Cinderwright, not in the woods clearing.
The road has a T junction there. A short spur runs south into a plaza with a campfire (it glows), a notice board, a covered wagon and two tents.
The caravan has moved here: its pen and both carts are at the rest area. Verdant no longer has a caravan yard.
The Acceleration Hop now has two runs, one each side of the T, each with green Landing tiles at its ends. No tiles sit on the junction.
Once a player has a home they spawn there instead. Nothing in the game sets a home yet; that comes with the home quest.

Acceleration Hop (issue 334)

Where: the world map (L_World_Factions_A), on the long straight road from the Verdant fountain toward Cinderwright.
Glowing hop tiles sit on its straights (the rest area T splits the road into two runs), two per 4 m road piece.
Step onto the road and a song starts. The tiles pulse on the beat.
Jump and land on a tile on the beat: a good landing adds 10% run speed, a perfect one 18%.
Downbeat tiles (pink) add 8% more when you land on the bar line. Boost tiles (orange chevrons) add 15% more.
Chain hops to stack it, up to 1.8x. Each boost lasts 4 beats and fades over 1. Sprint stacks on top; dash stays the same.
Landing tiles (green) at both ends hold your speed and close the chain. Miss a beat and the chain resets.
Walk off the road and the song stops after 3 seconds.

Beat Hop courses (sprint 3)

Where: hub gate into Beat Hop (L_Beat). On the start pad, the MAP row sits between DIFFICULTY and COURSE.
Free Hop is the old chart. The three new courses are open from the start.

Warm-Up Wobble (Easy, Pebble Parade, 96 BPM)
Teaches one thing at a time: hops, lane changes, a dash gate, then a 1.2 m mantle ledge.
The ledge is beside the lane. Jump the pad across the gap onto the landing hop.
Wide bricks, a checkpoint every 8 beats, and the catch floor under the pit.
Walk through the start gate. The pictogram board has no words. The name is the label above it.

Wall-Run Waltz (Normal, Fern Hop, 120 BPM)
Adds wall-run panels, grapple posts, swivel poles and crumble bricks.
Two-move combos: wall run into a dash gate, then later wall, dash and grapple.
Shown as Normal. The run is judged so swivel poles can clear.

Raptor Rush (Hard, Moonlit Moss, 140 BPM)
Everything above, plus beat walls on even beats and a 2.0 m ledge beside the lane.
The pad and the landing hop are the way across until mantling exists.
Tighter windows, a checkpoint every 32 beats, narrower bricks.

Song tiles: a song outside that course's BPM range is refused, with a line on the pad, and the pick stays.
Difficulty tiles stay put while one of these three is selected. Free Hop still uses them.
Each course has a hub return gate at the start and another after the finish. Home and the pause menu still work. Leaving mid-run asks first.

A finish at grade B or better pays materials once. Par is a score to show off, not a lock.
Best scores stay on this machine.

Dev builds only: gf.BeatHop.Course 1, gf.BeatHop.Course 2, gf.BeatHop.Course 3, and gf.BeatHop.Section <n> (1-based).
