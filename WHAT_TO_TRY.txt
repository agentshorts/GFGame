Beat Hop courses (sprint 3)

Where: hub gate into Beat Hop (L_Beat). On the start pad, the MAP row sits between DIFFICULTY and COURSE.
Free Hop is the old chart. The three new courses are open from the start.

Warm-Up Wobble (Easy, Pebble Parade, 96 BPM)
Teaches one thing at a time: hops, lane changes, a dash gate, then a 1.2 m mantle ledge.
The ledge is beside the lane. Jump the pad across the gap onto the landing hop.
Wide bricks, a checkpoint every 8 beats, and the catch floor under the pit.
Walk through the start gate. The pictogram board has no words. The name is the label above it.

Wall-Run Waltz (Normal, Fern Hop, 120 BPM)
Adds wall-run panels, grapple posts, swivel poles and crumble bricks.
Two-move combos: wall run into a dash gate, then later wall, dash and grapple.
Shown as Normal. The run is judged so swivel poles can clear.

Raptor Rush (Hard, Moonlit Moss, 140 BPM)
Everything above, plus beat walls on even beats and a 2.0 m ledge beside the lane.
The pad and the landing hop are the way across until mantling exists.
Tighter windows, a checkpoint every 32 beats, narrower bricks.

Song tiles: a song outside that course's BPM range is refused, with a line on the pad, and the pick stays.
Difficulty tiles stay put while one of these three is selected. Free Hop still uses them.
Each course has a hub return gate at the start and another after the finish. Home and the pause menu still work. Leaving mid-run asks first.

A finish at grade B or better pays materials once. Par is a score to show off, not a lock.
Best scores stay on this machine.
